Hosting and security

This website uses Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) for delivery and protection against attacks. It processes IP address, time, requested address and browser identification.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in a secure, available service. Cloudflare processes connection data to provide and secure its services; retention depends on the security purpose and contractual deletion rules. Technically necessary security cookies may be used under § 165(3) TKG 2021.

Cloudflare is a processor (Art. 28 GDPR). For transfers to the USA, Cloudflare relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR), supplemented by standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). Details: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy. The website and downloads are delivered directly by Cloudflare Workers.

EasyMySQL

Release requests and downloads use this website’s Cloudflare Worker. GitHub is contacted by the server, without forwarding your IP address. Only following an external link connects your browser to the respective provider. Your explicitly chosen language remains under “em-lang” in browser storage until you clear it. This provides your requested language setting (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; § 165(3) TKG 2021). The installed app also contacts this domain for updates, generating the connection data described above.

Using the app

Connections to your configured database, AI providers, Git services and development tool downloads depend on the functions and accounts you use. AI requests can send prompts, code and database context to the selected provider. These connections are made from your device and are governed by the respective provider’s privacy terms; they are not part of website hosting. Check which data you share before using them.

Contact by Email

When you write to me, I process your email address, your name and the content of your message in order to reply.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where it concerns a specific project, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The messages are stored on my own mail server at dataflair (Kurz & Lankow GbR, Germany). I delete them once the matter is settled, unless a statutory retention obligation applies.

Your Rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). These rights apply subject to their statutory conditions. You may withdraw consent at any time for the future. Contact details are available in the imprint. I normally respond to requests within one month under Art. 12 GDPR.

If you believe I am not processing your data lawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority. In Austria, this is the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde, dsb.gv.at).

There is no automated decision-making or profiling. You are not obliged to provide any data, but the sites cannot be accessed without the technically required connection data.
